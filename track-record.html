How scoring works

  • Pre-registration. I write the tests, catalysts, and exit conditions into the note before the outcomes are known. Scorecards grade those exact tables; I don't add criteria after the fact.
  • Kill-switches end positions, not price. Any two falsifiers from a note's exit conditions close the position regardless of where the stock trades, and I post the exit.
  • Mistakes stay up. A broken thesis gets a post-mortem, not a quiet deletion. The closed table is the point of this page.
