About

This site is where I put my own work on the record.

Schedule

A new piece goes up every Monday at 7:00 AM ET, written the night before. The first Monday of each month carries a full thesis; the other weeks carry macro reports, scorecards, and shorter notes. Event-driven scorecards (earnings, rulings) post the same day the facts arrive, outside the schedule.

The method

Each thesis commits to its evidence before the outcomes are known:

  • Pre-registered tests. Independent checks against filings, dockets, hiring data, credit markets, and employee signals, with bull and bear predictions written down before I look. Nulls and contradictions get reported, not filtered.
  • Dated catalysts. Every thesis names the events that will prove or refute it, and when. I register expectations before each event and grade them after.
  • Kill-switches. Exit conditions set at publication. When they fire, the position closes regardless of price, and I post the exit. Facts end positions; price doesn't.

The site's version history is public: I commit each post to a Git repository on the day it publishes, so the timestamps on pre-registered claims can be verified rather than taken on trust.
